Evidence library (18 improvements)

  • Vacant-lot greening: +4.3% after a year, +13% over six years within 1,000 ft (Philadelphia LandCare).
  • Street trees: ≈ +3% for the home fronting the tree, plus spillover to neighbors (Portland).
  • Green schoolyards: +2.5–5% nearby (LA, Denver, Boston and Philadelphia studies). LAUSD Measure US spends $8–12M per project.
  • Trails: the BeltLine added +18–27% faster appreciation within ½ mile, the 606 showed a 22% premium on the west end, and the Indy Cultural Trail gained ≈ $1B in assessed value on a $63M build.
  • Freeway caps: Pittsburgh ≈ +10% within a mile.
  • Demolitions: the evidence is contested. An early study claimed +4.2%; later work found ≈ $162 per nearby home.

Local research

Sourced notes for Carlson Park, Beverlywood and Palms, plus the 606, the BeltLine, Kensington, Fletcher Place, Klyde Warren and Brightmoor. Current local projects include the Ballona Creek path work (Apr–Oct 2026), the path extension (≈ $24.7M), Culver City's FY26-27 capital plan, the Venice Blvd lanes and the Inglewood Oil Field phase-out.

Funding climate. Most federal tree and greening money was frozen, cut or rescinded in 2025: USDA's urban forestry grants and DOT's Neighborhood Access program (≈ $3.1B clawed back). For now, local measures (Measure US, Measure W, Measure A), private money and HOAs are the realistic funders.

Read the numbers carefully

  • Value created isn't your profit. The scores count the gain to every nearby home. You capture only the share on property you own, or through taxes or assessments if you're a city.
  • The model is deliberately conservative but not yet calibrated. It uses the low end of each study, a ⅓ distance decay and estimated fit factors. Testing against past projects comes next.
  • "Vacant" in the Census mostly means homes for rent or sale. Blight fixes only rank where long-term vacancy is real and values and incomes are below the county median. That keeps vacant-lot greening out of Palms and Beverlywood.
  • Gaps: no crime data yet (lighting uses poverty as a stand-in); exact canopy % exists for only 466 tracts (the gap is used elsewhere); school scores are California-only; FEMA risk is missing.
