City Pretty Round 6 — Part A source audit
Access date: November 2, 2026

Scope

This pass examined A1-A5. The files contain 30 dataset/service candidates and
60 source references. Fifteen rows have recovered metadata or directory data;
15 are LISTED candidates whose target response or distribution is unresolved.
Actual three-record examples were recovered for two sources: the LA City parks
API and the Community Garden Council HTML directory. Other examples are NR.
No full park inventory, street network, or countywide completeness comparison
was downloaded. All requested LA feature counts and minimum park sizes remain
NR. Parts B, C, D, E, F and G were not reached. No zoning, legal, cost, rebate,
causal home-value, or benefit-valuation findings are claimed by this package.

VERIFIED means the stated metadata or records were opened and seen. It does
not mean the source has passed a GitHub Actions run, its licensing is cleared,
its current features are complete, or every column is known. LISTED means a
publisher/documentation/parent catalogue identifies it but target access or
distribution was not recovered. NR means not recovered. Source statuses and
dataset-row statuses serve different scopes; a verified policy page can
document a LISTED tile service. Search snippets were not promoted to retrieved
feature records. Date accessed describes this investigation; some retrievals
are cached representations with visibly different dates.

Part A notes

A1 — Streets

Use CAMS as the main official named-road candidate. The JSON exposes FullName
and the name components, local-city fields, source and update fields. The Type
field has no coded domain in the recovered schema. A functional road hierarchy
is therefore still unresolved; do not convert Type to a hierarchy by guessing.
The publisher identifies the data as cartography/geocoding, without routing
capabilities. [S001-S002]

NavigateLA supplies an official LA City alternative with STNAME, STSFX and
SFXDIR. It covers the City, not the entire County. TIGERweb has separate primary,
secondary and local road layers; Local Roads alone cannot fill the complete
street network. Its MTFCC and RTTYP fields are useful candidates for road class
and route type after querying the intended LA clip. [S003-S006]

Geofabrik publishes a Southern California PBF. The current listing reports
641 MB and a 2026-11-01T21:21:36Z cutoff. The binary response was not parsed.
ODbL attribution and redistribution conditions apply. Use OSM route relations
as well as road ways if deriving highway shields; no three LA route examples
were recovered. [S007-S012]

A2 — Basemaps and labels

Option                          Decision for City Pretty
OpenFreeMap                     Best documented keyless online candidate.
Protomaps self-hosted            Good architectural fallback; archive URL NR.
CARTO raster labels              Useful if a manually obtained key is acceptable.
Stadia Free                     Non-commercial only; finite credit allowance.
MapTiler Free                   Non-commercial/R&D only; authenticated, limited.
Esri legacy Light Gray          Avoid a new long-term dependency; retirement set.
USGS Topo                       Composite background, not a labels-only overlay.

These are recommendations from the recovered documentation, not successful
runtime tile tests. Raw tile templates that were not recovered are NR. Templates
with YOUR_KEY are documentation, not usable credentials. They do not meet the
strict no-credential source condition. [S013-S032]

OpenFreeMap documents unlimited online map views/requests and commercial use,
but its October 2026 terms restrict automated collection and bound individual
offline downloads. It is a display-service candidate, not permission to harvest
all its tiles in GitHub Actions. Required credits must remain visible. [S013-S016]

CARTO's raster API requires a key since September 25, 2026. Its September 29
terms allow 5 million free monthly tile requests for non-commercial use and
1 million for commercial use across all keys. Its FAQ says vector styles still
load without keys, while the key page instructs applying a key to both formats;
plan around a key and retain this discrepancy. The documented labels-only
styles can sit above colored polygons after a successful tile test. [S020-S023]

Stadia's free plan provides 200,000 credits per month and excludes commercial
use. MapTiler's free quota uses sessions or API requests under the applicable
client accounting; do not combine both quotas or call either unlimited.
MapTiler also restricts bulk tile export and proxying. [S024-S028]

Esri's notice puts World Light Gray Reference retirement in December 2029.
It is not already retired in November 2026. A retirement notice does not resolve
licensing. USGS Topo's service identifies public-domain source data but is an
opaque composite image; its separate licensing FAQ was not recovered. [S029-S032]

Implementation proposal, not a tested edit to the existing app: put subdued
basemap geometry below City Pretty's polygons, then park boundaries and road
lines, then text labels above them. A vector style can retain chosen text
symbols. Show major roads at county scale and add local road labels as users
zoom in. Do not promise every street name is simultaneously legible at county
scale. Fonts, sprites, tile CORS, mobile performance and attribution still need
a live integration check. No changes were made to the City Pretty HTML file.

A3 — Highway numbers and shields

The Caltrans SHN source is not currently cleared for a no-login collector.
An initial metadata representation was readable, but later requests redirected
to login; the alternate geodata endpoint returned JSON error 499, Token Required.
The catalogue itself could not be reopened. Do not turn a cached public listing
into a claim that the feature service is anonymously downloadable. A public
distribution or a successful runner test is still needed. [S056-S058]

OSM highway/ref/network documentation is the fallback research path. A road's
ref value alone may be insufficient to select the correct shield; retain route
network relationships and multiple-route memberships. This is an implementation
recommendation; the LA records and route-type value mapping are NR. [S010-S012]

A4 — Parks and open space

Recommended pair, explicitly an inference: use Countywide Parks and Open Space
as the primary inventory candidate, and CPAD as the second source for protected
open space and access categories. The county layer's fields support names,
reported/calculated acreage, agency, access and amenities. That makes it a useful
base, but no downloaded comparison established that it includes every public
park or every city. Remove the app's own 2-acre filter; the smallest actual
included polygon remains NR. [S033-S037]

Keep CPAD Open Access, Restricted Access, No Public Access and Unknown Access
distinct. CPAD describes protected, fee-owned land, so it cannot by itself
certify joint-use schoolyards, all plazas or all indoor recreation facilities.
Its units may have multiple noncontiguous holdings. The separate 2026a ZIP is
listed but returned 403; do not assume the accessible REST service is the same
vintage. Its recovered JSON says 2025b/December 2025, while an HTML representation
credits June 2026a. Actual service vintage remains unresolved. [S035-S038]

County DPR facilities are explicitly COUNTY PARKS ONLY. Their schema is polygon
although the description says points; inspect actual geometries before ingest.
STATUS distinguishes active, planned, undeveloped and archived facilities.
Original PNA polygons are a historical comparison source, not automatically the
current inventory. [S039-S042]

The county trail layers add named segments, amenities and entry points. Their
2013 copyright statements are not last-edit dates, and a trail segment is not
a whole trail. Current closures and full county trail completeness are NR.
[S043-S045]

Gap checks remain necessary for joint-use schools, gardens, small plazas,
beaches, trails, and newly opened sites. The LA City updated polygon endpoint
was not recovered. Community Garden Council entries do not imply unrestricted
walk-in access. The school-park list timed out. The PNA+ /8 school layer is an
opportunity layer, not evidence that public access agreements exist. Its /9
trail/bikeway layer still needs built-versus-planned fields. Safe Clean Water
project status and public access were not recovered. [S047-S053;S059]

OpenSpace_Public is a misleading name for an inventory search: its actual
catalogue contains Project Points, Project Lines, Project Areas and Project Site
Points. Exclude it from the base park inventory until its project records are
independently classified. [S054]

A5 — Amenities

The countywide layer offers concise counts such as PLGND, POOLS, TENIS, BSKTB,
DGPRK and RSTRM. County DPR adds detailed fields such as RESTROOM, SWIMMING_POOL,
COMMUNITY_GARDEN, PLAZA and DRINKING_FOUNTAINS. Keep unreported/null values
distinct from observed zero. Neither source's current reporting completeness
was verified. [S033-S034;S039]

The bare LA City Socrata JSON worked and supplied three records. Two records
describe 105TH STREET POCKET PARK at identical coordinates, once as Outdoor
Fitness and once as Parks; the third is 109TH STREET POOL. This demonstrates
why a facility/amenity row count is not a park count. Group duplicate locations
and keep amenity categories as a related set. The data.gov modified date is
2022-07-25, not proof of November 2026 field freshness. [S049-S050]

Machine access, identifiers and use rights

No source was executed in GitHub Actions during this session. Direct Python
HTTP checks from this environment failed with 403; that is evidence about this
host only. Web-retrieved metadata, cached pages and a source's public catalogue
are not a US runner success test. This is therefore a source audit with open
access gaps, not a production-ready list of guaranteed machine-accessible feeds.

verify_partA.py and verification_targets.json supply a read-only standard-library
check for the actual runner. They request schema, three records and a count,
save raw JSON when available, reject HTTP-200 ArcGIS error objects, and expose
the count's geography. National TIGER counts are labelled national. County DPR
counts retain their narrower ownership scope. Smallest positive stored acreage,
when obtainable, is not treated as an inclusion policy. No feature service is
edited, no API key is obtained, and the script never changes the research CSV.

Run from the unpacked package in the intended runner:

    python3 verify_partA.py --out checks_run1

Use a new output directory for a new run. For a quick subset:

    python3 verify_partA.py --only A1_CAMS,A4_COUNTYWIDE_PARKS,A5_LAPARKS_SOCRATA --out checks_run2

HEAD_ONLY for a file is deliberately not PASS: it establishes headers only,
not a complete valid download. A machine PASS does not establish licence rights,
full county coverage, current access hours, or current conditions.

All CSV cells are quoted UTF-8 text. Import identifier columns as text; Excel
automatic type inference can remove zeros even from quoted CSV. Keep AIN/APN,
TRA, GEOID and zone codes as strings in future parts. Three real directory/API
examples remain exactly as recovered, including unexpected address/city text.

The county eGIS terms page returned no readable text. Required reuse permission
and exact attribution for those feeds are NR; suggested credits are labelled
as suggestions. CPAD's state catalogue explicitly states Creative Commons
Attribution and no public-use restrictions, but the CC version was not recovered.
Publicly readable government data were not assumed to have a blanket licence.
[S009;S035;S046;S055]

Inferences about apparent nonexistence and restrictions

No absence was proven. Inference: the recovered sources do not establish one
complete, current, openly licensed countywide layer that combines every park,
joint-use agreement, garden, plaza, beach, trail and access schedule. The main
remaining work is record retrieval, deduplication, access validation and a
coverage comparison, not a claim that such a source cannot exist.

Do not plan around credential-free CARTO raster, unrestricted commercial use
of Stadia/MapTiler free plans, hotlinking Protomaps daily build downloads,
bulk harvesting OpenFreeMap tiles, the token-protected Caltrans endpoint, or
unverified public access to schoolyards and gardens. No paid report or paid
dataset was purchased. Monetary project costs and effect estimates were not
researched in this Part A pass.
