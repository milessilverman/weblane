A4. Direct GTFS feeds

All links are producer-hosted ZIP downloads or URLs serving ZIP content. The links were recovered from agency sources, Metro’s regional feed registry, or current feed-registry records. Not every ZIP was unpacked; calendar ranges are registry-observed rather than independently recomputed.

For every row, “GTFS tables” means:

  • stops.txt: one stop; stop_id, stop_lat, stop_lon.
  • trips.txt / stop_times.txt: trip and scheduled stop visit; route_id, trip_id, service_id, stop_sequence, arrival_time, departure_time.
  • Calendar tables: start_date, end_date, and/or exception date.
  • Where supplied, shapes.txt: shape_id, shape_pt_lat, shape_pt_lon, shape_pt_sequence.
