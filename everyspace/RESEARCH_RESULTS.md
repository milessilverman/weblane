# Everyspace: launch research and decision brief

Research cutoff: **November 4, 2026 (UTC)**. US focus. No launch city was supplied; the city shortlist is Phoenix, San Diego, and Indianapolis. This is a public-source research record, not a finding that every proposed use is permitted at every address.

**Evidence labels:** VERIFIED = stated in an official company, government, statute, or first-party offer; LIKELY = supported by secondary evidence but not confirmed in a current primary source; UNKNOWN = not established. A verified published offer is not proof that a particular booking qualifies. **n.d.** means no publication/update date was recovered from the retrieved page. Sources were checked November 3–4, 2026; copyright years and search crawl dates are not publication dates. Recommendations and calculations are explicitly identified. An UNKNOWN does not mean zero, permitted, prohibited, or unavailable.

**Important limits:** Several operators disclose fees only at checkout or in individual contracts. No defensible citywide price median was found for most space types, and no public standard rent was found for most infrastructure sites. Those gaps remain UNKNOWN. Authenticated contract schedules, parcel-specific zoning decisions, insurance policy forms, and a complete trademark clearance are outstanding diligence items.
